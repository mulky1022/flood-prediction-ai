Academic & Research Model Disclosure

Sri Lanka FloodWatch operates as a machine-learning research framework for early flood hazard detection, developed for the YCS Competition. Hydrological parameters are retrieved continuously via the Open-Meteo Historical & Forecast Weather API and calibrated against historical flood footprints recorded in the Kelani River Basin (Colombo District). The classification threshold utilizes a supervised Random Forest algorithm that incorporates upstream flow discharge, catchment rainfall volume, and local elevation indices.

Institutional Partner: University of Colombo / Research Lab • Lead Architecture: Random Forest Classifier Ensemble • Advisory: Official evacuation protocol remains governed strictly by the Disaster Management Centre (DMC) of Sri Lanka.
